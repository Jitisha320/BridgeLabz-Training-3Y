About Jaipur

Jaipur is the capital city of Rajasthan and is famous for its beautiful palaces, forts and colorful culture.

History

Jaipur was founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II. It is also known as the Pink City.

Famous Places

Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort, City Palace and Jantar Mantar are some of the famous attractions of Jaipur.
